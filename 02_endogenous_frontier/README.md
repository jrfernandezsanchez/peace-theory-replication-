Verification for "The Endogenous Peace Frontier" (SSRN 7248820).

verify_endogenous_frontier.py checks the grievance ceiling gmax/(1-e^-delta) that
installs bounded hardening (and confirms that the continuous-time bound gmax/delta
understates it), Theorem 1(a) and (b) — the infeasible set is bounded and its last
date t_2 respects the explicit ripeness bound — Theorem 1(c) (a single infeasible
interval under the concavity conditions), the four comparative statics of Theorem
1(e) at the crossing, Corollaries 1.1 and 1.2, Proposition 5 (the trap region is
non-empty iff lambda_D + rho*lambda_H > 1, upward-closed, and contains a
neighbourhood of certainty), and Theorem A1(c), which requires the grudge and the
silence thresholds jointly, implemented through the belief operator of Appendix A.
