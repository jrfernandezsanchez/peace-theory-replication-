Verification for "The Reconciliation Frontier" (SSRN 7248821).

verify_reconciliation_frontier.py checks Lemma 1 against numerical integration of
the memory ODE, Theorem 1(a) (delta* recovered by bisection on the raw definition,
not from the formula), Theorem 1(b) and (c) (the two regimes, and the reconciliation
date T against a simulated first passage), the comparative statics of Theorem 1(d),
the ratio result of Theorem 1(e), Proposition 2 (forgetting is not sufficient),
Propositions 3 and 4 by numerical differentiation, Proposition 6 (the closing
window, including the finite cutoff when the memory-free floor exceeds unity and the
transient feasibility that is lost at a finite date with no change in memory policy),
and Proposition 1, where symmetric and targeted enforcement are shown to behave
oppositely on the two sides of the frontier.
