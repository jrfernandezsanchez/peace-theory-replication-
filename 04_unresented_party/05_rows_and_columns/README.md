Verification for "Rows and Columns: Snidal (1991) in a Bargaining Setting"
(SSRN 7273658).

Scripts check that Snidal's normalization gives constant row sums equal to r and
hence spectral radius r, that the feasibility threshold is r <= 1 at every n
under uniform weights, and the three-state example of Section 7 (r = 1.5,
feasible division x = (0.4, 0.4, 0.2)).
