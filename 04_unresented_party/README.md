Verification for "The Unresented Party: Spite Networks, Feasibility, and the
Incidence of Multilateral Surplus" (SSRN 7270178).

Scripts check spectral sufficiency, the unresented-column theorem, the Farkas
certificate of infeasibility, the irreducible counterexample to spectral
necessity, and the exact rescue condition, against a linear-programming oracle
on randomized instances with a fixed seed.
